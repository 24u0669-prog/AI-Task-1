Selected: Logistic Regression
Logistic Regression is typically stronger on recall for the minority churn class. Random Forest is often slightly better on overall accuracy. For a retention campaign, missing a churner (false negative) is more costly, so F1 and recall were prioritized over accuracy.

Responsible AI

Stratified splits and class_weight="balanced" were used because churn is ~26.5% of the base. customerID was dropped. Gender and SeniorCitizen should be fairness-audited before production. Do not store raw PII in model artifacts.
